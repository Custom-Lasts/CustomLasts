# Custom Lasts

Public atelier site for [customlasts.app](https://customlasts.app).

Consumers scan a foot with iPhone camera + ARKit (no LiDAR) into a digital last. Wearers free. Makers subscribe. Native iOS is a separate app — this repo is the marketing, privacy, and support site.

## Brand
- Cream `#F4EFE6`
- Espresso `#1C1916`
- Sage `#3F5C4D`
- Tagline: Your last. Forged from your foot.
